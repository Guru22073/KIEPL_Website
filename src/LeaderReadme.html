My Leadership Philosophy

I believe that effective leadership is about empowering teams to achieve their full potential. My approach focuses on:

  • Trust and Empowerment: I trust team members to own their work and make decisions.
  • Transparency: I communicate openly about the what, why, and how of our work.
  • Growth Mindset: I view challenges as opportunities to learn and improve.
  • Strategic Vision: I focus on connecting our daily work to the bigger picture.

My role is to remove obstacles, provide context, and ensure we're all aligned on our objectives. I expect ownership, honesty, and a commitment to continuous improvement from my team.

Feedback

Feedback is a gift that helps us all grow. Here's my approach to feedback:

How I Give Feedback

  • Timely and specific, focused on behaviors and impact
  • Balanced between strengths and areas for improvement
  • Private for constructive feedback, public for praise
  • Ongoing rather than saved for formal reviews

How I Prefer to Receive Feedback

I welcome direct, honest feedback about my leadership and decision-making. The best ways to provide me feedback are:

  • During our 1:1 meetings
  • Via email if you prefer to collect your thoughts in writing
  • Anonymously through our quarterly team survey

I promise to listen with an open mind, ask clarifying questions, and take appropriate action. I don't expect perfect delivery - the intent to help me improve is what matters most.
